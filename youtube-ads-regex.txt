# =============================================================================
#  Pi-hole REGEX deny rules: Google / YouTube ad networks (every subdomain)
# =============================================================================
#  Author:  Russell Collis   Version: 1.0.0   Updated: 2026-11-22
#
#  Adlists can't hold regex rules. Add these as regex deny entries instead:
#    Pi-hole v6:  Domains > "Regex filter" > Deny, or
#                 sudo pihole --regex '<pattern>'
#    Pi-hole v5:  Blacklist > "Add regex", or  pihole --regex '<pattern>'
#
#  Each pattern matches the root domain AND every subdomain:
#  (^|\.) means "start of the name, or a dot", and $ means "end of the name".
# =============================================================================

# DoubleClick: ad creatives, VAST video-ad XML, click tracking
(^|\.)doubleclick\.net$
# DoubleClick rich-media and video ad file host
(^|\.)2mdn\.net$
# AdSense / display and video ad delivery
(^|\.)googlesyndication\.com$
# Conversion tracking and Sponsored-link redirects (breaks clicking Google Sponsored results)
(^|\.)googleadservices\.com$
# Google Publisher Tag loader
(^|\.)googletagservices\.com$
# adservice.google.<any country TLD>, e.g. adservice.google.co.uk
^adservice\.google\.[a-z]{2,3}(\.[a-z]{2})?$
# YouTube's own legacy ad hosts
^ads?\.youtube\.com$
# Third-party ad verification (Moat, IAS, DoubleVerify)
(^|\.)moatads\.com$
(^|\.)adsafeprotected\.com$
(^|\.)doubleverify\.com$
